# KuPlanner · KuPlannerV7.0 — User Guide

## Start

Use the planner in your browser, or download KuPlannerV7.0.zip. Right-click the ZIP, choose Extract All, then open **OPEN KuPlannerV7.0.html**. Keep the `_planner_files` folder beside that file.

No account or app installation is required. Your plans and Campus Passport progress stay in your current browser on this device. Moving folders, switching browsers or clearing browser data may separate or remove saved data. Use **Export my plan** to keep a JSON copy of your plans.

## KuPlannerV7.0 interface

KuPlannerV7.0 refreshes the interface while keeping all four setup steps and the existing planner features. Use the left sidebar on a computer, or the menu button on a phone, to open your planner, priorities, study goal, reminders and campus tools. The weekly planner scrolls horizontally on smaller screens. Dialogs scroll independently when needed. Motion follows your device’s reduced-motion preference. The included fonts work offline.

## Build your week

Complete the study-goal, personal-activities, course-schedule and task questions. Add classes and labs manually, or choose **Import schedule photo**. Check the suggested study sessions before adding them to your week. Manual plans can be edited at any time.

## Import a timetable screenshot

Choose a PNG, JPG, WebP or BMP screenshot under 12 MB, choose its first week, then press **Read my schedule**. Include weekday headings and the left time axis when possible. For a screenshot with cropped-out weekdays, confirm the first weekday column when asked. Do not guess if the image lacks that information.

Review every course name, weekday, start/end time and lecture/lab category before adding the entries. Time estimates use the visible block edges and time axis; the reader cannot reconstruct labels that have been cropped away. The downloaded edition includes its text reader and can read screenshots without internet.

## Browser banner

The sidebar provides access to your planner and campus tools. There are no app-install prompts.

## KU Campus Passport

Open **KU Campus Passport** from the navigation menu. Enter your preferred name, collect stamps by completing challenges, or skip a challenge for a points penalty. Complete all eight stamps to receive a certificate and increase your streak. Use **Back to planner** to return.

## Offline use

The downloaded planner and Passport open locally. The hosted browser planner prepares offline files after its first connected visit. KU website links need internet. The downloaded edition includes its screenshot reader and works without an internet connection.


## Course and campus tools

- **Book a study room:** opens the official KU Library booking website in a new tab. Internet required.
- **Life Points leaderboard:** a feature preview using fictional students and sample points. It does not rank real users. Your existing Passport points stay local.
- **Import course syllabus:** upload a PDF with selectable text, DOCX or TXT (up to 20 MB), or paste the assessment table. Reading happens on your device. Scanned or password-protected PDFs may need a text copy.
- Review the detected course and each possible quiz, assignment, exam or milestone. Select **Yes, choose date & time**, enter a date and time, then select **Add to my schedule**. **No, skip** adds nothing. You can edit the title and preparation estimate.
- Dates and week numbers shown in the syllabus are hints, not confirmed calendar dates. Check with your instructor. General categories (such as weekly homework) appear once; they do not create every occurrence. Detection may miss items. Add missing tasks with **Add a plan**.
- Use the complete extracted ZIP for offline syllabus reading. In the online version, the PDF/DOCX reader becomes available offline after its first successful use.


## Weekly focus tools

- **Weekly priorities:** unfinished assignments due in the displayed week appear in High, Normal, Low order. Within a priority, earlier deadlines rank first, then larger preparation estimates. Change priorities from the list; completed tasks leave the list.
- **Skip for now:** check the first Monday of teaching week 1 in syllabus import. Select Skip for now, confirm the review Monday, then Save for later. No calendar event is created. Saved items appear in the review list when that week arrives, including missed weeks when you next open the planner. Show all saved allows early review. Add date & time still requires explicit confirmation. Review next week postpones a decision; Skip this item dismisses it. Undated assessments require you to choose a review week.
- **Study reminders:** daily local reminder time defaults to 18:00, plus nudges 15 minutes before planned study sessions. Change or pause them under Reminder preferences. Snooze and dismiss are saved on this device. Optional browser alerts ask for permission only when you click Enable browser alerts on the secure website. Keep the planner open; a closed page cannot deliver scheduled reminders.
- Export my plan now includes saved syllabus suggestions, priorities and reminder preferences. Clear local data removes them with the rest of the planner data. Campus Passport progress stays in its existing separate storage.


## Version 7 updates

The new sidebar workspace uses KU blue, white and subtle gold accents. Mobile navigation supports keyboard focus and Escape. The online version caches the new interface and fonts for offline reopening; PDF/DOCX reader files are cached after first use. The ZIP includes all readers and fonts. Study reminders still require an open planner.

Use **Manual entry** to add lectures, labs, assignments, quizzes, midterm exams, final exams, study sessions and personal plans. Quizzes and exams use a date, start time, optional end time and preparation estimate. They participate in priorities, task reminders and suggested preparation just like assignments.
