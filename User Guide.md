# KuPlannerV5.0 — User Guide

## Start

Use the planner in your browser, or download KuPlannerV5.0.zip. Right-click the ZIP, choose Extract All, then open **OPEN KuPlannerV5.0.html**. Keep the `_planner_files` folder beside that file.

No account or app installation is required. Your plans and Campus Passport progress stay in your current browser on this device. Moving folders, switching browsers or clearing browser data may separate or remove saved data. Use **Export my plan** to keep a JSON copy of your plans.

## Build your week

Complete the study-goal, personal-activities, course-schedule and task questions. Add classes and labs manually, or choose **Import schedule photo**. Check the suggested study sessions before adding them to your week. Manual plans can be edited at any time.

## Import a timetable screenshot

Choose a PNG, JPG, WebP or BMP screenshot under 12 MB, choose its first week, then press **Read my schedule**. Include weekday headings and the left time axis when possible. For a screenshot with cropped-out weekdays, confirm the first weekday column when asked. Do not guess if the image lacks that information.

Review every course name, weekday, start/end time and lecture/lab category before adding the entries. Time estimates use the visible block edges and time axis; the reader cannot reconstruct labels that have been cropped away. The downloaded edition includes its text reader and can read screenshots without internet.

## Browser banner

The small information banner has an **×** button. Closing it stays remembered in that browser. There are no app-install prompts.

## KU Campus Passport

Open **KU Campus Passport** at the top of the planner. Enter your preferred name, collect stamps by completing challenges, or skip a challenge for a points penalty. Complete all eight stamps to receive a certificate and increase your streak. Use **Back to planner** to return.

## Offline use

The downloaded planner and Passport open locally. The hosted browser planner prepares offline files after its first connected visit. KU website links need internet. The downloaded edition includes its screenshot reader and works without an internet connection.
